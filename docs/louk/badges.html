Images are re-compressed for the repo (posters at the server's 580 px, backdrops at 1280 px) so expect slightly softer text than a live render. Dark layout colour as in the AIOStreams app.

Backdrops as the app's Home hero (1280 x 576)

With the app's shades (left-to-right dark side shade, dark bottom shade; safe-areas.md). The hero box is 1280 x 576 (36 rem, the height on a 1280 x 720 TV), image cover-fitted and top-aligned, so the bottom 144 px of the picture are cropped; the app dims the badges together with the picture, so a grey figure low in the column is the shade, not the render. On a title's own page the app removes both shades. Rendered at 1920 px, shown at 1280 px. Per look: the default right-edge column, and the horizontal top-right row (badge_direction=h) that the safe-areas doc recommends on TV. The disc look has no row: the two images are identical.
